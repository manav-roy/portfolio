My story

I learnt to sell first. Then I learnt why selling works.

01
2022 – 2023 · Jaro Education

The hard start

My first two months in sales: zero deals, bottom of the leaderboard. Over that year I spoke to more than 30,000 prospects and heard “no” from 99.5% of them.

The roughly 100 who said yes added up to ₹1 Cr in revenue, 115% of my annual target and a place among the top performers in my branch. Consistency got me there. The craft of selling and persuading is what made me want to understand sales properly.

Lesson: rejection is data. Volume plus learning beats talent.
02
2024 – 2026 · Goa Institute of Management

Sharpening the edge

To learn the strategy behind the selling, I did a PGDM in Marketing & Strategy at Goa Institute of Management. It sat right where my interests meet: how businesses grow, and a campus surrounded by the outdoors I love.

There I was a finalist (12 of 1,200) at the IIT Roorkee case competition, a D.E. Shaw Fin Quest Fellow (1 of 14), and built a go-to-market plan for Havells from 410 retail outlets and 100 customer interviews.

03
Apr 2026 – Present · InsuranceDekho

Owning the number

From selling one MBA programme at a time to the central sales strategy team, owning a 17,000-partner portfolio worth ₹80 Cr a month. I take full ownership of the work:

Personal project · Claude Code, GitHub, Vercel

Pipeline Desk: an AI job-search system

The problem: applying through one-click portals brought only rejections, and I kept finding good roles too late.

What it does: Python scouts pull fresh roles from 39 company career boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday) and job sites. Each role is scored against my profile with a 70% match gate and paired with the right CV. A scheduled cloud agent refreshes it every two hours, and Gmail sync updates application status on its own.

Python scrapersATS public APIsMatch scoringRole-specific CVsGmail syncScheduled cloud agentFollow-up sprintMulti-user login

Result: it showed me that applying on company sites converts far better than portals, and changed how I search.

The dashboard is private because it holds personal application data.
